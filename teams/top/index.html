What do these numbers mean?
Team score
The overall rating, 0–100, higher is better. A weighted blend of six parts: threat cover 30%, defensive typing 15%, offensive typing 10%, bulk 15%, safety 15% and consistency 15%.
Threat score
PvPoke's measure of how vulnerable the team is to the league's six most dangerous counters, from simulated battles with 0 and 1 shields. Lower is better.
Pts.
The chip under each Pokémon: its PvPoke role score in this league, out of 100. Lead is how well it opens the match, Switch how safely it comes in mid-battle, Closer how well it finishes once shields are gone.
