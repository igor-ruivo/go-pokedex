Find Pokémon Worth Trading

A third, separate question from the two tabs above: which of your catches are worth handing off in a trade? A Best Friend trade always floors every stat at 5. In this mode, a species just needs to clear ONE of the four cutoffs below (Great, Ultra, Master, or Raid) to be suggested. Master League and raids have no CP cap, so any relevant species qualifies on rank alone. Great and Ultra are pickier: their CP caps often mean the best possible spread for a species needs very low Attack, which a trade’s floor of 5 can never produce — so those two leagues only count a species when its own best spread is actually something a trade could reach. Either way, a catch that’s already perfect — or already tied for the best possible spread in whichever league qualified it — is never suggested, since trading it could only lose it, never improve it. The categories, whitelist, and CP cap below narrow the suggestions further, same as the other tabs.
